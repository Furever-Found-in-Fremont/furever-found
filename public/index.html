Rescue. Rehab. Rehome. Community.

Helping animals. Supporting people. Building community.

We are a small, volunteer-run rescue in Riverton, Wyoming, caring for dogs and cats across Fremont County until they find the place they can call home.

Meet adoptable animalsHelp us help them

Rescued with patience

Our volunteer rescue team goes out themselves, often onto the Wind River Reservation, to coax frightened animals into safety. Many arrive stressed or traumatized, and helping them heal is how we work from the first day to adoption day.

Forever found day

When an adoption is final, new owners bring their animal’s forever collar, and we swap it for the rescue collar. It is how we say the search is over.
