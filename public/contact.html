Visiting

Visits are by appointment only, so please call first. We’re generally available 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., seven days a week.

Found an animal or have an emergency?

We’re not an emergency service, but we’ll do our best to point you in the right direction. For an animal emergency, contact local animal control or the police. You can also call us at (307) 414-3516 at any hour, day or night. The line forwards to whoever is on call, and we’ll help connect you with the right agency, such as the Riverton Police Department or Lander Animal Control.

We can’t accept owner surrenders; please contact local animal control.
