Adopt

We match animals with homes carefully, because every animal here is looking for a forever one.

How adoption works

  1. Application. Tell us about you, your home, and your household.
  2. Review. We read every application and may follow up with questions.
  3. Meet-and-greet. Meet the animal in person, with any other pets or family members who will share the home.
  4. Home check. We visit to make sure the home is a safe fit.
  5. Contract. We sign the adoption contract, and you take your new family member home.

Start your application We aim to respond within 24 to 48 hours.

Forever collar. On pickup day, bring a collar for your new animal. We replace the rescue collar with the forever collar as part of the handoff.
