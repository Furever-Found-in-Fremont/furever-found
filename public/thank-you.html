Thank you for applying!

We’ve received your application, and we’re so glad you want to open your home to one of our animals. Every application is read by a real person on our small volunteer team.

What happens next

  1. Review. We read your application and may call or email with questions. [Typical response time, to be confirmed by Amanda.]
  2. Meet-and-greet. If it looks like a good fit, we’ll set up a visit so you can meet the animal, along with anyone else who will share your home.
  3. Home check. We visit to make sure the home is a safe fit.
  4. Contract. We sign the adoption contract, and you take your new family member home.

Because we’re volunteer-run, please be patient with us. Questions in the meantime? Call (307) 414-3516 or email info@fureverfound.pet.
