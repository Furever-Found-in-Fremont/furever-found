Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Furever Found in Fremont, Inc. (“Furever Found in Fremont,” “we,” “us”) is a nonprofit animal rescue at 921 Linda Lee St, Riverton, WY 82501. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit an adoption, foster, or contact form, we collect what you enter: for example your name, email, phone number, address, household details, and information about your pets and home.
  • Donation information. If you donate online, your payment is handled by a payment service such as Venmo, or others we may add. We do not see or store your full card or account number. We may receive your name, email, and the donation amount so we can thank you and keep records.
  • Basic technical information. Our website is hosted on Cloudflare, which may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

We use your information to review applications, contact you about animals and events, process and acknowledge donations, keep required nonprofit records, and keep the website secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the rescue and website, and only as needed. These currently include PawPlacer (our animal and adopter records system), Cloudflare (website hosting and security), Zoho (email), and the payment services you choose when donating. We may also disclose information if required by law. Adoption and foster applications may be shared with our reviewers and, for reference or home checks, with people you list.

How long we keep it

We keep adoption and foster records for as long as needed to manage placements, follow up on animals we have placed, and meet legal and nonprofit recordkeeping requirements. You can ask us to correct or delete your information, though we may need to keep some records.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Adoption and foster applicants must be adults.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop emailing you, by contacting us below.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above.
