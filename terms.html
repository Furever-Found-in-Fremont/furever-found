About this site

This site shares information about Furever Found in Fremont, the animals in our care, our events, and ways to help. We try to keep it accurate and current, but we cannot guarantee that every listing, status, photo, or event detail is up to date. An animal shown as available may already be pending adoption.

Adoptions and fosters

Submitting an application does not guarantee an adoption or foster placement. All placements go through our process (application, review, meet-and-greet, home check, and contract) and are at our discretion, with the animal’s welfare as our priority. Fees and requirements are described on our adoption page and in the adoption contract, and may change.

Surrenders, found animals, and emergencies

We do not accept owner surrenders because we must keep space for animals we rescue; please contact local animal control. This website is not an emergency service. For an animal emergency, contact local animal control or the police. You can also call us at (307) 414-3516 at any hour; the line forwards to whoever is on call, and we will help connect you with the right agency.
